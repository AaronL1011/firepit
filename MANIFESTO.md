# Fire, AI and the Firepit.

Fire, its origins interpreted varyingly across cultures, objectively was the first revolutionary change in humanities ability to leverage energy, power, and heat into the industrial and utility applications we have today. Held in hands, bounded by a vessel, wielded by its creator.

AI in a similar sense, can be thought of as humanities next technological revolution - to leverage thought, decision and language into equally industrial and utilitarian form. This is another emergence of a highly powerful, and much like fire, potentially highly dangerous tool - depending on how it is wielded.

AI then, requires its own form of a complimentary tools for those who desire to wield it: fire pits, torches, chimneys and extinguishers etc. Simple, versatile, bounded vessels for the existence of AI to operate within, granting the users of the fire pit with safe, controlled and predictable operation.

This is the high-level goal of Firepit AI. Open, honest and simple standards of tooling for AI operation within broader society, BYO fire.

Containment of AI as it looks today is one of two forms; **expert-only infrastructure** in the form of sandboxes and containers, often out of reach of newer technologists and the lay man - or **vendor-granted policy**, usage policies and guard rails dictated by those who sell you their fire.

These centralised patterns of governance, safety and control do not scale fairly across a broader society, often falling over in accessibility, democracy and trust. They cannot promise to serve everyone fairly or in a way where AI benefits humanity as a whole, as opposed to a select few.

Containment, utilisation and auditability of AI must be owned by and transparent to the user. The boundary of danger belongs to the one who bears the risk, not the provider of AI.

For humanity to wield emergent technology properly, we must develop the tools to harness it. We must define the bounds of where it can spread, how hot it can get, and when it's time to put it out. Just like our ancestors, once again we are called back to primitive ideas to help us control this new fire of intellect.

- Akin to a pit, we must maintain a bounded edge of visibility and prevention of spread.
- Like the torch, we want a way to selectively harness the power to apply it intentionally in a space while maintaining a grasp on it.
- As with ignition of great power, also comes the ability to extinguish and smother at will.
- Any great fire leaves its signature within its smoke - no fire is unseen, every trail can be audited.
- A chimney can define directed egress and controlled output.
- When worst comes to worst, a failed pit collapses in, self-smothering in the process.

It's these primative principles that inspire and guide Firepit AI. The goal is not a safety product, the goal is not governance or alignment. We want to help humanity find the tools and principles that our ancestors did when they were handed fire. We want to ignite the new era of accessible and trustworthy AI.
